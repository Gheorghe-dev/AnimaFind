## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the tactile romance of an expedition journal fused with the crisp, rewarding micro-interactions of a contemporary creature-collection mobile game. It speaks to nature enthusiasts, specimen collectors, and casual field explorers who relish discovery, observation, and personal codex progression.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Tactile Field Naturalist Skeuomorphism**. Rather than sterile flat glass, surfaces emulate textured sun-cured parchment, worn linocut specimen tags, deckle-edged pressed paper, and patinated brass fasteners. The UI balances antique scientific expedition documents with dynamic, saturated taxonomic signifiers—giving every creature card, biometric dial, and field observation note the weight and permanence of an authentic physical artifact.

Key emotional pillars:
- **Scholarly Wonder:** The intimacy of documenting an unseen species in a weathered leather-bound compendium.
- **Physicality & Weight:** Buttons depress with physical travel; tags affix via washi adhesive or brass grommets; cards pivot with holographic prismatic foils.
- **Ecological Precision:** Clean, legible anatomical hierarchy paired with warm botanical parchment tones.

## Colors

The palette operates across three distinct chromatic strata: the **Parchment Bedrock**, the **Field Greens**, and the **Taxonomic & Holographic Prisms**.

### Core Foundation
- **Canvas Base (Sunlit Parchment):** `#FAF6EE` serves as the primary viewport surface.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Pressed Kraft / Codex Page):** `#F3EBDD` provides contrasting containment for specimen boards, field plates, and scroll containers.
- **Surface Layer 3 (Deep Manila / Card Pocket):** `#EADDC9` used for depressed slots, inset wells, and inactive card cradles.
- **Deep Moss Slate (Primary Ink):** `#2B3A2C` grounds the primary typography, hand-inked borders, and structural contours.
- **Specimen Green (Secondary Structural):** `#3F5E4D` defines botanical accents, specimen headers, and primary active indicators.

### Taxonomic Biome Highlights
- **Emberwood Terracotta:** `#C85A32` denotes diurnal, desert, and volcanic habitats; also used for urgent notifications and alert badging.
- **Hedgerow Olive:** `#5A7D42` marks common flora, riparian wetlands, and field guide status indicators.
- **Meadow Emerald:** `#2E8540` indicates discovery affirmations, rare biological traits, and active stamina recovery.
- **Twilight Indigo:** `#3B4CCA` reserved for nocturnal, abyssal, and celestial species classes.

### Holographic Foil & Energy Shimmers
- **Aurora Amber Foil:** `#F59E0B`
- **Glacial Cyan Foil:** `#06B6D4`
- Applied exclusively as 35° dynamic gradient masks on apex-tier field discovery stamps, holographic card rims, and chromatic specimen seals (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B 0%, #06B6D4 50%, #F59E0B 100%)`).

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges historical Linnaean taxonomy publications and responsive mobile ergonomics:

- **Display & Headings (Vollkorn):** A robust, bookish serif with warm, organic terminal serifs that retain legibility on backlit retina screens while honoring Victorian field notes. Use sentence case for naturalist field entries and title case for creature denominations.
- **Interface & Descriptive Prose (Work Sans):** Modern grotesque sans with crisp horizontal terminals. Carries all variable-length biological lore, habitat guides, and critical data tables without visual fatigue.
- **Taxonomic Metadata & Specimen Stamps (Courier Prime):** A typewriter-monospaced register that mimics specimen catalog tags, accession numbers, latitude/longitude markings, and capture timestamps. Always styled uppercase when used in badges, index seals, and washi-tape labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for **portrait mobile ergonomics** with rigid safe-zone integration for native iOS and Android wrappers (Capacitor/Cordova):

- **Column System:** 4-column fluid layout on mobile viewports (<640px) with 12px gutters; expands to an 8-column layout on tablet split-screens (641px–1024px).
- **Physical Journal Page Margins:** A dedicated 16px screen-margin inset on mobile, preserving a visible parchment border around active screen content to simulate codex page leaves.
- **Vertical Base Unit:** An 8pt spatial grid govern all heights, pads, and vertical offsets. Sub-elements (icon bounds, stamp counters) snap to a 4pt micro-grid.
- **Thumb Reach Architecture:** High-frequency tactical game actions (Can opening, Card flicking, Codex filter toggling) sit within the bottom 38% of the viewport height. Informational lore and taxonomy metadata populate the upper visual zone.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through physical paper and material stratification rather than generic, diffuse blurred drop-shadows:

- **Tier 0 (Codex Folio / Deck Background):** Flat parchment canvas `#FAF6EE` with subtle 2% noise texture to mimic cold-pressed cellulose.
- **Tier 1 (Parchment Sheets & Ledger Slates):** Resting elevation. `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.08), 0 3px 6px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.04)`. Outlined with a 1.5px solid border in `#E3D5C0` mimicking scored paper edges.
- **Tier 2 (Specimen Plates & Raised Cards):** Resting specimen plates. `box-shadow: 0 4px 10px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.12), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Outlines use hand-inked variable strokes (`#2B3A2C` at 85% opacity).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Mechanical Controls & Rations):** Tactile Skeuomorphic Energy Cans, bottom navigation docks, and active drag targets. `box-shadow: 0 8px 18px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.18), 0 2px 4px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.12)`.
- **Depressed / Cut-Out States (Specimen Card Silhouette Wells):** Inverted elevation for uncollected codex entries. `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 6px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.18), inset 0 1px 2px rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.25)`.
- **Brass Hardware Accents:** Rivets, ring binders, and eyelets cast crisp, short directional shadows: `1px 2px 2px rgba(30, 20, 10, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an organic, artisanal roundedness (`0.5rem` standard border-radius) that emulates manual paper-cutting, corner-clipped card mounts, and vintage bookbinding corners.

- **Standard Cards & Ledger Boxes:** 8px (`rounded-md`) corner radii.
- **Field Badges & Specimen Tags:** 4px clipped or filleted corners (`rounded-sm`), often augmented with a simulated 4px circular punch-hole (brass eyelet) centered at the top edge.
- **Pills, Washi Tape, & Floating Pills:** 16px to 24px (`rounded-full`) pill form. Washi tape variants feature jagged zig-zag or diagonal edge masks (`clip-path`) on their left and right extremities.
- **Doodle & Hand-drawn Borders:** Key focus components swap straight geometric borders for subtle SVG-filtered wavy line boundaries (simulating micron pens on rough paper: 1.5px to 2px stroke weight, `#2B3A2C`).

## Components

### 1. Tactile Skeuomorphic Energy Can (🥫)
- **Geometry:** Cylindrical pill proportion (48px × 76px) positioned floating bottom-right or within inventory grids.
- **Visuals:** Brushed tin metal top and rim, wrapped in a paper label featuring retro botanical line-work. Employs a glossy vertical cylinder reflection highlight gradient.
- **State Changes:** When pressed, translates 3px downward (`transform: translateY(3px)`) with instant haptic vibration and an audible "hiss-pop" ring-pull visual sequence. A numerical stamina fill-level is displayed across the label in Courier Prime.

### 2. Creature Specimen Cards & Holographic Sheen
- **Anatomy:** 3:4 aspect ratio portrait frame. Exterior frame is textured kraft paper (#F3EBDD) secured with brass eyelet corner studs. Inside lies a circular or arch-topped creature illustration window.
- **Holographic Tier Animation:** For legendary and rare catches, an interactive gyroscope/pointer-driven shimmer overlay runs diagonal light rays: `mix-blend-mode: color-dodge` using the Aurora Amber to Glacial Cyan gradient (`#F59E0B` to `#06B6D4`).
- **Uncollected State:** Inset embossed deboss well with silhouette ink wash and a faint question mark rendered in Vollkorn.

### 3. Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Field Button:** `#3F5E4D` fill with a bottom 3px offset border (`#2B3A2C`) producing a physical push-button switch feel. Label is centered in Work Sans 14px SemiBold in `#FAF6EE`. On active press: zero shadow and a 2px downward displacement.
- **Secondary Field Note Button:** Bleached kraft (#FAF6EE) with a 1.5px sketchy doodle contour `#2B3A2C`. Text in `#2B3A2C`.
- **Washi Tape Button:** Semi-translucent colored banner (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.85)` or `#5A7D42`) with subtle paper fibers, rotated between -1.5° and +1.5° randomly to preserve hand-placed field scrap aesthetics.

### 4. Chips & Taxonomic Field Badges
- **Status & Class Badges:** Rendered as miniature linen tags with monospaced Courier Prime text (11px).
- **Rarity Badges:** Outlined with a 1px stitched dashed line (`border: 1.5px dashed #2B3A2C`).
- **Filter Chips:** Parchment pills with `#FAF6EE` background when inactive; when selected, stamped with an inked Meadow Emerald `#2E8540` background and botanical leaf insignia.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios, & Specimen Stamp Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** Resemble square field-journal checkboxes (18px × 18px) with slightly uneven line weights. On selection, a hand-drawn botanical checkmark (or ink "X") animates inside via an SVG stroke-dasharray transition.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular brass watch-dial or specimen specimen pin-cap design.

### 6. Input Fields
- **Search & Log Inputs:** Inset grooved parchment fields (`#EADDC9`) with an interior shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(43,58,44,0.1)`). Placeholder text mimics faded grey pencil writing (`rgba(43, 58, 44, 0.5)` in Work Sans Italic). Focus state transitions border to a solid `#3F5E4D` line with a brass-eyelet search icon at the leading edge.

### 7. Native Mobile Bottom Navigation Dock
- **Structure:** Floating dock pinned to bottom safe area inset. Fixed height 64px, resting on `#FAF6EE` parchment slab with a top hand-drawn separator line (`#2B3A2C` 1px).
- **Navigation Items:** 4 primary destinations (Codex, Field Map, Expedition Log, Camp/Inventory).
- **Active State:** The active item receives an authentic circular botanical stamp ring (36px diameter, colored in Terracotta `#C85A32` or Meadow Emerald `#2E8540`) with an organic ink-bleed edge and a micro-elevation bounce.
